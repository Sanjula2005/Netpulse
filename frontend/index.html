How it actually works

Think of it like a doctor reading your pulse. They don't need to open you up — just the rhythm of your heartbeat tells them something is wrong. We do the same with network packets.

1
Listen passively

We timestamp every packet arriving on the network. We never read the contents — this works on fully encrypted traffic like HTTPS, Zoom, or WhatsApp calls.

2
Measure the rhythm

The gap between packets (called "inter-arrival time" or IAT) creates a rhythm. Normal traffic has a steady beat. Congestion makes it irregular — like an arrhythmia.

3
AI reads the pattern

A Temporal Convolutional Network (TCN) looks at the last 100 packet gaps and predicts: will there be congestion in the next 2–5 seconds? It catches subtle patterns that simple averages miss.

4
Act before the freeze

Instead of waiting for the call to buffer (reactive), the app drops video quality before congestion hits (proactive). The user sees a smooth 720p instead of a frozen 1080p.
